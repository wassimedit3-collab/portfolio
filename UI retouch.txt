### 9. Skills — Color by Percentage
- Added `--pct` custom property to each `.cloud-tag` (e.g., `--pct:99`)
- CSS `color-mix()` dynamically computes:
  - **Text**: blends between `--text-muted` (at low %) and `--text-white` (at high %)
  - **Border**: blends between `--border` (at low %) and `--accent` (at high %)
- 99% (Photoshop, Lightroom) → nearly full red
- 27% (Motion Graphics) → mostly muted gray
- Wrapped in `@supports (color: color-mix(in srgb, red, blue))` for browser fallback
